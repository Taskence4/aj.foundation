# Website V3 implementation

Implemented from `AJ_Foundation_Website_V3_Final_Content_Developer_Handoff.pdf`.
Public copy is stored in `src/data/v3-pages.json` and `src/data/v3-home.json`;
handoff notes, publication restrictions and placeholders are not rendered.
The existing brand colours, fonts, routes and static-export configuration are retained.

## Delivery and launch details

- Enquiry forms use the existing `mailto:` delivery route. Required fields, email
  format, radio selection and consent are validated. Opening a draft is not proof
  of receipt, so the confirmation tells the visitor to send it in their email app.
  The handoff's receipt confirmation requires a real submission service and should
  only appear after that service confirms successful receipt.
- Existing contact details (`info@ajfoundation.org`, `+91 80500 89834`) are retained.
  The PDF supplies placeholders rather than replacements; official ownership and
  availability still need organisational confirmation before launch.
- Registration, office, founder, governance, effective-date and unapproved legal
  details remain unpublished. Add policy effective dates at the actual launch.
- Confirm the deployed host/email provider, enquiry retention arrangements and
  responsible privacy contact before public launch. The application contains no
  analytics, tracking cookies, upload control or submission storage.
- Existing article images remain explicitly labelled representational. No image
  is presented as a documented Foundation programme or founder photograph.
  Confirm asset rights before publishing; source licence records were not provided
  in the handoff.
- Accessibility text states an intended standard and planned formal review, not
  a claim of independently audited compliance.
